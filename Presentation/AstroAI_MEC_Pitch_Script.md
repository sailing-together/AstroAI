# AstroAI 3-Minute Pitch Script

**Speakers:** Speaker 1, Speaker 2
**Total Time:** ~2 minutes speaking + ~1 minute demo = 3 minutes

---

### (Slide 1: Title)

**(0:00 - 0:15)**

**Speaker 1:** We all want a little more clarity in our busy lives. But the wellness tools we have today often just create more noise.

We’re AstroAI — a thoughtful companion that transforms astrology from a passive curiosity into gentle, actionable guidance you can use every day.

---

### (Slide 2: The Problem)

**(0:15 - 0:30)**

**Speaker 2:** Young people are turning to astrology for guidance, but current apps fail them. They give vague advice like "be careful with money" or "you may feel tension." This isn't helpful, it's just noise.

The core problem is a lack of specific, actionable guidance that connects to their real lives.

---

### (Slide 3: The Solution)

**(0:30 - 0:45)**

**Speaker 1:** AstroAI is different. We provide specific, personalized, and actionable advice. Instead of just telling you *what* might happen, our AI-powered engine helps you understand *what you can do about it*.

---

### (Slide 4: Live Demo)

**(0:45 - 1:30)**

**Speaker 2:** Let me quickly show you how it works.

**(DEMO - 45 seconds)**
*   **(1) Show Daily Insight:** "Here is my personalized insight for today. Notice it doesn't just describe a feeling; it gives me a small, actionable step to take."
*   **(2) Show Event Analysis:** "Now, for our key feature. I can ask it to analyze a future event, like a job interview. AstroAI gives me concrete suggestions, like communication tactics to use and even the best times of day for me to be at my sharpest."
*   **(3) Show Natal Chart:** "And all of this is powered by my unique natal chart, which we make simple and beautiful to understand."

---

### (Slide 5: The Vision)

**(1:30 - 1:50)**

**Speaker 1:** As you can see, AstroAI is already more than a horoscope app. But our vision is much bigger. We are building an **ecosystem of wellness.** By understanding our users' needs, we can connect them to a curated marketplace of high-value services—like professional therapists, meditation coaches, and wellness travel, creating a powerful new revenue stream.

---

### (Slide 6: Our Plan to Win)

**(1:50 - 2:05)**

**Speaker 2:** We’ll capture the market of Gen Z and Millennials who are tired of generic tools. We’ll grow through a points and referral system that encourages viral sharing, and we have a clear roadmap: first, launch and perfect the core AI, then build our community, and finally, expand our wellness ecosystem.

---

### (Slide 7: The Ask)

**(2:05 - 2:15)**

**Speaker 1:** We are seeking the MEC prize to accelerate this vision. These funds will fuel our user acquisition and allow us to build the foundation for our community and partnership platform.

---

### (Slide 8: Thank You)

**(2:15 - 2:20)**

**Speaker 2:** Our goal is to build the indispensable AI companion for the next generation.

**Both:** Thank you.
